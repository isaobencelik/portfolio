Job Analyser

A tool that turns a long list of job postings into a short list worth applying to.

The problem

Job hunting on LinkedIn is mostly reading. Every search returns dozens of long postings, written in similar language, and the useful parts are buried: what the role is really for, what is truly required, and what it pays. Most of that time goes on postings you would never apply to.

Who it's for

Anyone actively looking for a job, starting with me. I built it for my own search in Lisbon, which made me both the product owner and the first user: every annoyance was a backlog item.

The solution

  • →Live search of public LinkedIn listings, automatically widened to related job titles and nearby towns
  • →An AI breakdown of each posting: why the role exists, must-haves vs nice-to-haves, skills, seniority and culture
  • →Red and green flags, perks, and a salary estimate calibrated to the local market
  • →A job library that keeps everything you've looked at, with export

The value

  • →Time: seconds to understand a posting instead of minutes
  • →Focus: effort goes into the few roles that fit, not the long tail
  • →Clarity: salary and red flags visible before you invest in an application
  • →Reach: roles you'd miss because they use a different title or sit in the next town

Key product decisions

From personal tool to public demo

It started on my own laptop. Opening it to the public meant rescoping: owner-only features like settings and auto-collect are switched off in the public version.

Guardrails on cost and fairness

AI calls are the real cost, so every visitor has rate limits and there is a daily AI budget cap. One heavy user can't run up a bill or slow it down for everyone else.

Small, cheap, always shippable

It runs on a serverless setup that scales to zero when idle and deploys automatically from GitHub, so improvements go live in minutes and hosting costs stay close to zero.

What's next

Salary estimates for more markets than Portugal, a "paste a job description" mode that doesn't depend on LinkedIn, and storage that keeps your job library between visits.
