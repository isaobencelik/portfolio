Master's Project · Healthcare NOVA IMS

Helf: a patient-owned healthcare companion

Briefing

Patients leave a consultation with a sheet of instructions, new medications and more questions than they came with. The system was never built with them in mind, so they fill the gap with Google, online groups and AI chatbots. It is not one bad day. It is every appointment.

Decision

Stay on the patient's side. Fitness-first products track the body, but none help you inside the healthcare system, where you feel most vulnerable. Helf needs nothing from the doctor, so it works with any doctor from day one.
