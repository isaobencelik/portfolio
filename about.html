About me

Hi, I'm Oben.

I'm a Product Owner based in Lisbon, working on customer experience across European markets.

My work covers RPA programmes, omnichannel standardisation and knowledge management, in a hybrid Product Owner and Product Manager role inside a SAFe environment.

Before product, I spent years in IT infrastructure and support, including as a Microsoft Support Engineer. That is where I learned how systems break, and how it feels for the people on the other end when they do.

I hold the SAFe® POPM 6.0 certification and I'm completing a Master's in Information Systems Management at NOVA IMS. In my spare time I build small apps, like Job Analyser.
